How the course works

  • Go in order. Each module assumes the ones before it.
  • Every module ends with a quiz. Eighteen scenario questions are drawn at random from a larger bank, so retakes differ. The pass mark is 72%, like a certification exam, and memorising definitions is not enough to pass.
  • Read the explanation for every option, including the ones you got right. The wrong options are where the misconceptions live.
  • "Explain it back" prompts appear before each quiz. Say the answer out loud or write it down before opening the model answer.
  • Two final exams of 65 questions with a 130 minute timer mix every module. Take Final A after the last module, and Final B a week after passing A.
  • The "Core path" switch at the top of each module hides the labs, the "explain it back" prompts, further reading and other deep-dive material when you are short on time.
  • Progress is stored in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. Use the Reset link in the sidebar to start over.
